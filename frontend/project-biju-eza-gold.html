Premium Commercial Showroom

The Eza Gold showroom in Thrissur represents an exquisite project in custom retail space planning by AR Constructions & Realtors. Built for Mr. Biju, the showroom combines safety, structural flow, and visual distinction to match the luxury character of gold and diamond collections.

Display zones feature customized hardware fittings, secure tempered glass dividers, and warm backlighting that highlights the jewelry sets. The client seating areas balance modular soft upholstery with premium timber tables and dividers to create a comfortable customer experience.

Lighting is calibrated to provide optimal visual illumination. Precise task spotlights align with display trays, complemented by custom ambient ceilings that diffuse light softly across the entire retail floor, eliminating glare and shadows.
