Modern Commercial Bakery Cafe

The Navya Bake House cafe layout represents a contemporary, food-retail design project executed by AR Constructions & Realtors. Tailored for Kurian & Hitha, the space features a highly organized counter grid, custom display glass cabinets, and cozy cafe dining areas.

Special focus was given to food hygiene and materials selection. All surfaces feature food-grade composite quartz and treated solid timber accents, offering high longevity and easy maintenance. Display lighting illuminates the baked goods shelves without generating heat.

The seating plan features compact, space-efficient wood furniture designs and custom divider panels to maximize seating capacity while maintaining smooth traffic flow for grab-and-go shoppers.
